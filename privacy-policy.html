Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 13, 2026

1. Introduction & Scope

At Liquid Labs Inc. ("Liquid Labs", "we", "our", or "us"), we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. Liquid Labs Inc. is an Ontario, Canada-based software consultancy and mobile application publisher.

This Privacy Policy explains our privacy practices regarding information collected through our corporate website (liquidlabs.ca), direct communications, and consulting inquiries. It also outlines the relationship between this website policy and the dedicated privacy policies governing our individual mobile applications.

2. Mobile Applications & Dedicated App Policies

Liquid Labs Inc. develops and publishes mobile applications for Android (available via the Google Play Store) and open-source software libraries. Because our mobile applications operate with different permissions, device capabilities, and data architectures (most being offline-first, on-device, or privacy-focused), each application maintains its own dedicated, standalone Privacy Policy.

For details on on-device data processing, permissions, and third-party API interactions for specific apps, please review their respective privacy policies:

  • Snippet — Code Notebook & AI: Snippet Privacy Policy (Offline-first, encrypted DataStore, zero telemetry)
  • CodeMateX — AI Coding Tutor: CodeMateX Privacy Policy (100% on-device AI execution, zero cloud LLM logging)
  • Android Device Universe: Android Device Universe Privacy Policy

  • Weather Alert: Weather Alert Privacy Policy
  • TRMNL Buddy: TRMNL Buddy Privacy Policy
  • TRMNL Display: TRMNL Display Privacy Policy
  • Remote Notify: Remote Notify Privacy Policy
  • Keep Alive: Keep Alive Privacy Policy
  • H.K. Vision — Wallpapers: H.K. Vision Privacy Policy

3. Information We Collect on This Website

We collect information through the website in two ways:

A. Information You Voluntarily Provide

When you submit a project inquiry via our contact form or contact us directly by email (contact@liquidlabs.ca or privacy@liquidlabs.ca) or phone, you provide personal information which may include:

  • Identity & Contact Details: Name, email address, organization/company name, and phone number.
  • Message Contents: Project requirements, technical specifications, or feedback included in your correspondence.

We use this information exclusively to communicate with you, respond to your inquiries, and evaluate consulting engagements. We do not sell, rent, monetize, or trade your contact information, and we do not add your contact details to unsolicited marketing newsletters.

B. Technical Log Data & Static Hosting

Our website is statically generated and hosted via GitHub Pages (provided by GitHub, Inc. / Microsoft Corporation). When you visit liquidlabs.ca, standard web server requests are processed by GitHub's infrastructure and content delivery network (CDN).

These logs may include your Internet Protocol (IP) address, browser type and version, operating system, referring URL, and request timestamps. This data is collected and processed by GitHub for security, network diagnostics, DDoS mitigation, and system integrity in accordance with the GitHub Privacy Statement.

4. Cookies & Local Storage

We prioritize visitor privacy and strive to keep our website lightweight and non-intrusive:

  • No Advertising or Tracking Cookies: We do not deploy third-party advertising cookies, cross-site trackers, or behavioral remarketing pixels on this website.
  • Functional Local Storage: We use browser localStorage (specifically the key cookiesAccepted) solely to remember whether you have acknowledged our cookie and privacy notice, avoiding displaying the banner on subsequent visits.

You can configure your web browser to block or alert you about cookies or clear local storage through your browser settings at any time.

5. Third-Party Services & External Links

Our website may contain embedded elements or links to external services:

  • Embedded Maps: We embed an interactive location map via Google Maps (Google LLC) on our Contact section for geographical convenience. Interaction with the map is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.
  • External Links: We provide links to external websites and services, including Google Play Store, GitHub repositories, and social networks (such as X / formerly Twitter). We do not control and are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of third-party websites.

6. Children's Privacy (COPPA)

The Liquid Labs Inc. corporate website is intended for general commercial and developer audiences and is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through this website.

For our child- and family-focused applications (such as Math Pup Tutor), we strictly adhere to the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). Please consult the Math Pup Tutor Privacy Policy for full details regarding parental controls, zero personal data collection, and child safety.

7. Data Security & Retention

We implement appropriate technical and administrative safeguards to protect your personal data against unauthorized access, loss, or alteration. All web traffic to liquidlabs.ca is encrypted in transit using Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS).

Inquiries and business communications are retained only as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes for which they were collected, maintain business records, or comply with legal obligations.

8. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction (such as under Canada's PIPEDA, the European Union's GDPR, or the California Consumer Privacy Act / CCPA), you may have specific statutory rights regarding your personal information, including:

  • The right to request access to the personal data we hold about you.
  • The right to request correction of inaccurate or incomplete personal data.
  • The right to request the deletion or erasure of your personal data.
  • The right to withdraw consent to processing previously granted.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact our privacy representative at privacy@liquidlabs.ca.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, legal requirements, or services. The revised policy will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. We encourage you to periodically review this page.
