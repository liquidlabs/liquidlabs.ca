Privacy Policy for Workbook: E-Ink & Print PDF

Last updated: November 23, 2026

Welcome to Workbook: E-Ink & Print PDF ("the App"), developed by Liquid Labs Inc. Your privacy is our highest priority. This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles information on Android.

1. 100% Offline & Zero Data Collection

Workbook: E-Ink & Print PDF does not collect, transmit, store remotely, or share any personal or usage data:

  • No Network Permissions: The App does not request the INTERNET permission and cannot connect to external servers.
  • No Accounts or Sign-In: The App requires no registration, email address, username, or profile.
  • No Ads or Analytics: The App contains zero advertisements, zero analytics SDKs, and zero crash-reporting or telemetry trackers.

2. Local Device Storage

All worksheet settings and generated files remain strictly on your device:

  • Preferences: Workbook configuration settings (such as selected grade level, ruling size, font, theme mode, and page size) are stored locally in the App's private Jetpack DataStore storage.
  • Generated PDFs: Exported PDFs are saved locally to your device (for example, in Downloads/Workbooks/) or shared via Android's FileProvider only when you explicitly choose to open in a PDF reader, print wirelessly, or share through the system Share Sheet.
  • Uninstalling the App removes its locally stored preferences from your device.

3. Children's Privacy

While the generated worksheets are designed for Kindergarten – Grade 3 students, the App itself is a parent and teacher utility and collects no personal information from any user, including children under the age of 13.

4. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected with an updated date on this page.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions or feedback regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us via email at contact@liquidlabs.ca.
