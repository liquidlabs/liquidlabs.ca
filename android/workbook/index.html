Workbook — E-Ink & Print PDF Worksheet Generator

A lightweight, 100% offline Android utility for parents and teachers to generate custom primary school PDF workbooks (Handwriting, Math, Draw & Write Story Pages, Shapes & Patterns, and Telling Time) for Kindergarten – Grade 3 students. Designed for e-ink tablets (Onyx BOOX, Bigme, Meebook), Android tablets, large-screen foldables, ChromeOS, and wireless home printing.

5 Workbook Subjects

✍️ Handwriting (Triple-Lined Primary Ruling)

Enter custom spelling words, sentences, or paragraphs with automatic word-wrapping, or load K–3 presets (CVC words, Alphabet A–Z, Sight Words, Proverbs & Science Facts). Includes 3 bundled offline school fonts (Standard Print, Dotted Primary, Slanted Manuscript) and 3 practice modes (Hybrid, Trace Over, Copy Under) in 16mm, 12mm, 10mm, and 8mm line heights.

➕ Math & Number Sense

Generate Addition & Subtraction Equations (Horizontal or Vertical Stacked), Multiplication Times Tables (1×–12×), Missing Number Equations, Number Bonds (Part-Part-Whole), and Skip Counting Sequences (1s, 2s, 5s, 10s). Supports configurable ranges (Within 10, 20, 50, 100), 1-tap deterministic shuffle, and an optional Answer Key page.

📖 Draw & Write Story Pages

Configurable top picture drawing frame (Half Page or Top Third) paired with grade-aware story starter prompts and calibrated triple-lined handwriting rows filling the bottom of the page.

🔷 Shapes & Patterns

Practice fine motor skills with Traceable Geometric Shapes, Pre-Writing Line & Pattern Tracing tracks, Symmetry & Mirror Grids, and Numbered Dot-to-Dot constellations.

🕒 Telling Time (Analog Clocks)

Crisp vector analog clocks supporting Read the Clock and Draw the Hands modes across Hours, Half-Hours, Quarter-Hours, and 5-Minute intervals, with an optional Answer Key page.

Designed for E-Ink, Tablets & Home Printing

  • 🖋️ Native E-Ink Stylus Handoff: Export crisp vector PDFs and open directly in your tablet's native Notes or PDF Reader app (such as BOOX NeoReader) for hardware-accelerated (~25ms) EMR stylus writing.
  • 🖨️ Wireless Printing & Sharing: Print directly to Wi-Fi home printers via Android Print, share via the system Share Sheet (BOOX Drop, Google Drive, Email), or save to Downloads/Workbooks/.
  • 📐 Two-Pane Adaptive Workspace: Side-by-side configuration and live 1:1 WYSIWYG worksheet preview on tablets and large-screen foldables (>= 600dp).
  • 📄 Flexible Page Layouts: Supports US Letter and A4 in both Portrait and Landscape orientations with an optional Name & Date header.
  • 🖤 Zero-Animation High-Contrast UI: Light Paper White and Dark Inverted Black Material 3 themes with zero navigation animations to prevent e-ink ghosting.
  • 🔒 100% Offline & Private: Zero network permissions, zero ads, no accounts, and local-only preferences.
